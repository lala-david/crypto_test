## 🇺🇸 Briefing

**Liquid Network loses $320M; OFAC sanctions Xinbi Guarantee. 27 new incidents today**

- **[Liquid Network](https://www.chainalysis.com/blog/320m-exploit-liquid-network/)** (Liquid, Bitcoin, Ethereum, Sep 6) — The attacker exploited a secret-transaction verification cache bug, minted L-BTC without collateral and peg-out roughly 4,000 BTC ($320M). 3,400 BTC have been returned while about 600 BTC remain in the attacker’s wallet pending recovery.
- **[Tectonic](https://rekt.news/tectonic-rekt)** (Cronos, Ethereum, Aug 30) — The attacker performed 98 circular loans, inflating TONIC price up to 195x and borrowing $120.4M across nine pools. Validators rolled back 10,961 blocks, recovering $111.2M; $9.19M bridged to Ethereum remains unrecovered and was split into Tornado Cash.
- **[Unknown Gnosis Safe Wallet](https://x.com/SlowMist_Team/status/2099779127662493875)** (Ethereum, Sep 15) — By abusing a Router multicall privilege bypass, the attacker delegate-called the Safe module, fed aEthrsETH into a malicious Uniswap V4 hook and swapped it for rsETH. Roughly $7.73M was stolen; Kelp has paused the receiving address for 24 h, with no recovery reported yet.
- **[Nomic nBTC Bridge](https://x.com/osmosis/status/2097623097696251926)** (Osmosis, Nomic, Sep 9) — A custom forwarding bug allowed double-spending of nBTC and sending collateral-free vouchers to Osmosis. 22.65 BTC in the attacker’s wallet have been frozen on-chain, and governance plans to confiscate them, covering the shortfall from the community pool.
- **[Symbiosis](https://x.com/symbiosis_fi/status/2098384161673285716)** (Ethereum, BSC, Rootstock, Bitcoin, Sep 11) — Exploiting a BridgeV2 parsing bug and negative fee, the attacker minted syBTC without collateral and sold ~4.39 WBTC on Uniswap V4 for $336K. The overall loss is $775K; the team has shut the bridge and secured about 15.2 BTC, offering a 20% bounty.
- **[Xinbi Guarantee](https://www.chainalysis.com/blog/ofac-sanctions-xinbi-cybercriminal-crypto-marketplace/)** (Tron, Ethereum, BSC, Sep 9) — The U.S. OFAC sanctioned Xinbi Guarantee and its tech partners for laundering over $52M of hacked funds. A total of 186 addresses were frozen or seized, with 133 address(es) already on our blacklist.
- **[Malone Lam](https://cointelegraph.com/news/malone-lam-pleads-guilty-245m-crypto-theft-conspiracy)** (—, Sep 9) — Malone Lam pleaded guilty to a $245M crypto-theft conspiracy, using social engineering and home invasions to target victims. He is awaiting sentencing.
- **[ORB](https://github.com/SunWeb3Sec/DeFiHackLabs/blob/main/src/test/2026-09/ORB_exp.sol)** (BSC, Sep 11) — Using flash loans to bulk-buy ORB and a re-entrancy bug in the burnLP loop, the attacker siphoned ~45 BNB ($32.6K). 11 address(es) already on our blacklist. The flash loan was fully repaid and profit taken.
- **Small incidents** — Spiral (Ethereum, Sep 14) $26.8K, YamFinance (Ethereum, Sep 12) $121K, Dominion (Solana, Sep 11) $238K, BeatXswap (BSC, Sep 9) $63.7K net profit, EtherFiAtomicQueue (Ethereum, Sep 9) ~15.45 ETH, OrderFactory (Ethereum, Sep 8) ~24.7 ETH, EnsoFinance (Ethereum, Sep 9) ~5.6 ETH, OMNI404 (Ethereum, Sep 9) $5.9K. 23 address(es) already on our blacklist.
