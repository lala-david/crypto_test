## 🇺🇸 Briefing

**Liquid Network loses $320M; 28 new incidents today**

- **[Liquid Network](https://www.chainalysis.com/blog/320m-exploit-liquid-network/)** (Liquid, Sep 6) — The attacker exploited a secret transaction-validation cache identification flaw, mapping invalid data to a previously approved result and minting L-BTC without collateral, then peg-out about 4,000 BTC (≈$320M). The flaw stemmed from reusing cached approvals instead of fresh verification. About 3,400 BTC has been returned; the remaining ~600 BTC stays in the attacker’s wallet pending recovery.
- **[Tectonic](https://rekt.news/tectonic-rekt)** (Cronos·Ethereum, Aug 30) — The attacker supplied $5M as collateral, borrowed TONIC repeatedly in a circular loan 98 times, inflating its price up to 195x and borrowing $120.4M across nine pools. The loop lacked proper collateral-value checks. Cronos validators rolled back 10,961 blocks, recovering $111.2M; $9.19M on Ethereum remains unrecovered.
- **[Nesa](https://rekt.news/nesa-rekt)** (Ethereum·Cosmos, Aug 24) — A Cosmos EVM accounting bug caused underflow in locked vesting accounts and overflow in others, allowing the attacker to move roughly 257.7 M NES (≈$50M) through the Hyperlane bridge to Ethereum. The bug failed to verify balances during accounting. The attacker attempted large sales on CoW Protocol and Uniswap V4 but suffered heavy slippage, reducing realized profit.
- **[Xinbi Guarantee](https://www.chainalysis.com/blog/ofac-sanctions-xinbi-cybercriminal-crypto-marketplace/)** (Tron·Ethereum·BSC, Sep 9) — OFAC sanctioned the Chinese-Telegram-based guarantee marketplace for laundering hack proceeds and operating a “Black U” coin-swap service, freezing over $52M of crypto assets. The platform facilitated laundering of hacking funds. 186 addresses were blocked, and 133 address(es) already on our blacklist.
- **[Malone Lam](https://cointelegraph.com/news/malone-lam-pleads-guilty-245m-crypto-theft-conspiracy)** (—, Sep 9) — The defendant pleaded guilty to a $245M crypto-theft conspiracy using social engineering and home invasions to target high-net-worth asset owners. Direct victim profiling enabled wallet compromises. Sentencing is pending while authorities continue tracing the proceeds.
- **[Unknown Gnosis Safe Wallet](https://x.com/SlowMist_Team/status/2099779127662493875)** (Ethereum, Sep 15) — The attacker bypassed Router’s multicall permission, delegate-called the Safe module, injected aEthrsETH into a malicious Uniswap V4 hook pool and swapped it for rsETH before withdrawing. The exploit arose from insufficient permission checks. Approximately $7.73M was drained; no recovery has been reported yet.
- **[Chainflip](https://x.com/Chainflip/status/2099080597281145040)** (Tron, Sep 12) — By attaching arbitrary memos to already-signed validator transactions, the attacker fooled the system into treating a failed same-deposit swap as a duplicate refund, executing eight attacks that paid out $736,442.17 USDT. The memo handling logic lacked validation. $115,654.41 USDT remains safely in the vault while a patch is being prepared.
- **[ORB](https://github.com/SunWeb3Sec/DeFiHackLabs/blob/main/src/test/2026-09/ORB_exp.sol)** (BSC, Sep 11) — Using flash-loaned funds the attacker bought large amounts of ORB, then exploited a re-entrancy flaw in the refund logic to repeatedly burn LP tokens, draining the pool and stealing about 45 BNB (≈$32.6K). Absence of re-entrancy protection enabled the attack. The loan was fully repaid; 47 addresses were involved and 11 address(es) already on our blacklist.
- **Small incidents** — Long Bridge (Robinhood Chain, Sep 14) $118K WETH auto-withdrawal was halted and the vault was re-funded, no user loss; Spiral (Ethereum, Sep 14) $26.8K loss from spot-price manipulation and tx.origin bypass; OMNI404 (Ethereum, Sep 9) $5.9K loss via ERC-404 dual-interpretation bug, 4 address(es) already on our blacklist; BeatXswap (BSC, Sep 9) $63.7K net profit from flash-loan price manipulation, 3 address(es) already on our blacklist; EtherFiAtomicQueue (Ethereum, Sep 9) ≈$15.45 ETH stolen through missing solver access control, 3 address(es) already on our blacklist; OrderFactory (Ethereum, Sep 8) ≈$24.7 ETH loss due to missing order-creation verification, 1 address(es) already on our blacklist; EnsoFinance (Ethereum, Sep 9) ≈5.6 ETH loss from TWAP manipulation, 1 address(es) already on our blacklist.
- **Takeaway** — Most of the incidents this week exploited missing price or balance verification, or permission checks, allowing attackers to inflate collateral or bypass access controls. Protocols should adopt multi-source price feeds, enforce re-entrancy guards, and tighten permission validation on critical functions.
