## 🇺🇸 Briefing

**PPP·EIDL fraud of $245M uncovered; federal investigation expands. 1 new incident today**

- **[Select Decks / Vin Phat](https://www.justice.gov/usao-ndwv/pr/northern-district-west-virginia-joins-doj-fraud-division-sba-and-sba-oig-surge)** — Defendants fabricated fake businesses, inflated payroll and revenue, and stole identities to obtain PPP and EIDL loans. The false documentation and stolen identities allowed the loans to be approved, which is why the scheme succeeded. A total of $245M was moved into vehicle purchases, cryptocurrency investments, and personal accounts, and 40 district prosecutors are now charging over 160 individuals. James Baldwin received a 30-day sentence and a $1.0M restitution order, while Kurt Ly has been indicted.
- **Takeaway** — Large-scale PPP/EIDL fraud using fake businesses and identity theft continues to surface. Monitoring fund flows into crypto investments and personal spending is essential.
