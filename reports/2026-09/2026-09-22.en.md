### 1. Internet Token — Hack / Exploit

- **Source**: [Internet Token — Smart Contract Vulnerability](https://x.com/DefimonAlerts/status/2101969009461567690) (SlowMist Hacked, 2026-09-21)
- **Incident date**: 2026-09-21  |  **Chains**: Base  |  **Amount**: $265.0K
- **Tags**: Access Control, Arbitrary External Call, Smart Contract Vulnerability
- **Other sources**: [DeFiLlama Hacks](https://defillama.com/hacks)

**Background**  
Internet Token DAO runs an INT/WETH pool on the Base chain. Its LiquidityUnifier contract was designed to trust a caller-supplied Uniswap V3 pool address. This design flaw allowed an attacker to register a fake pool.

**Attack / Modus Operandi**  
The attacker supplied a malicious pool address and triggered a fake Uniswap V3 pool callback. The fake pool minted about 925 million INT out of thin air. Then the attacker withdrew 5.847 WETH from the official INT/WETH pool and kept roughly 764 million INT.

**Summary**  
On Sep 21 2026 the attacker exploited the LiquidityUnifier vulnerability and stole assets worth $265K. A fake pool callback minted a large amount of INT and the attacker withdrew 5.847 WETH from the legitimate pool. The attacker kept about 764 million INT and submitted a governance proposal to move treasury funds. The mint role has not been revoked, and copycat exploits have inflated supply further.

**Fund Flow**  
Fake pool callback → 925 M INT minted → 5.847 WETH withdrawn from official pool → 764 M INT retained.

### 2. RWC Token — Hack / Exploit

- **Source**: [RWC Token — Smart Contract Vulnerability](https://x.com/TenArmorAlert/status/2101925758214316524) (SlowMist Hacked, 2026-09-21)
- **Incident date**: 2026-09-21  |  **Chains**: BNB Smart Chain  |  **Amount**: $109.5K
- **Tags**: Smart Contract Vulnerability

**Background**  
RWC Token is an ERC-20 compatible token deployed on the BNB Smart Chain. It provides liquidity on PancakeSwap in an RWC/USDT pair and has a fee logic that sends a portion of each transfer to the project’s company and platform wallets. The pool’s smart contract contains an unprotected burn function that can be called by anyone. The attacker leveraged a flash loan to exploit this function.

**Attack / Modus Operandi**  
The attacker used a flash loan to burn RWC tokens from the PancakeSwap RWC/USDT pool and called sync() to rewrite the pool’s reserves, inflating the price before selling the tokens for USDT.

**Summary**  
On Sep 21, 2026 the attacker exploited the PancakeSwap RWC/USDT pool with a flash-loan attack and stole about $109,460.85 in USDT. Approximately $39,964.07 was kept by the attacker, while $69,496.78 was sent to the project’s company and platform wallets through the token’s fee logic. The loss occurred in a single transaction.

**Fund Flow**  
Of the stolen USDT, $39,964.07 went to the attacker’s wallet and $69,496.78 was sent to the project’s company and platform wallets through the token’s fee mechanism.
