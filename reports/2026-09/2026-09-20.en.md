### 1. Blink Wallet — Hack / Exploit

- **Source**: [Blink Wallet — Unauthorized access to custodial accounts](https://x.com/blinkbtc/status/2101290680584695956) (SlowMist Hacked, 2026-09-19)
- **Incident date**: 2026-09-19  |  **Chains**: Bitcoin  |  **Amount**: -
- **Tags**: Unauthorized access to custodial accounts

**Background**  
Blink Wallet is a custodial Bitcoin Lightning wallet service. Users store funds in Blink Wallet and use the Lightning Network for payments. The service manages a limited number of accounts centrally.

**Attack / Modus Operandi**  
The attacker gained unauthorized access to custodial accounts, likely by compromising credentials or exploiting internal systems, and withdrew funds from the compromised accounts.

**Summary**  
On Sep 19, 2026 a security incident occurred at Blink Wallet. An attacker accessed a limited number of custodial accounts and withdrew funds, but the majority of assets remained secure. Non-custodial wallets were not affected. The team is conducting an in-depth investigation and recovery.

**Fund Flow**  
The attacker withdrew funds from the compromised custodial accounts; the destination of the funds has not been disclosed.

### 2. BitBank — Sanctions

- **Source**: [[모닝 뉴스브리핑] 미 재무부, 이란 연계 비트뱅크 제재 外](https://www.tokenpost.kr/news/briefing/410898) (rss:tokenpost, 2026-09-19)
- **Incident date**: 2026-09-17  |  **Chains**: Bitcoin  |  **Amount**: 수억달러
- **Actors**: Babak Zanjani, OFAC
- **Tags**: 브리핑
- **Follow-up — first reported**: [2026-09-18 · BitBank](https://www.trmlabs.com/resources/blog/ofac-sanctions-bitbank-expanding-action-against-babak-zanjanis-digital-asset-network)

**Background**  
BitBank is part of a digital-asset sanction-evasion network linked to Iranian financier Babak Zanjani, and was used to move billions of dollars in Bitcoin to the Iran Revolutionary Guard.

**Attack / Modus Operandi**  
OFAC designated BitBank for facilitating Bitcoin transfers that evade sanctions, and Blink Wallet reported that an attacker accessed a limited number of custodial accounts and moved funds.

**Summary**  
On Sep 17, 2026 OFAC sanctioned BitBank for aiding Iran’s sanction evasion. The exchange allegedly transferred billions of dollars in BTC to the IRGC. At the same time Blink Wallet said some custodial accounts were breached and funds were moved, and the company pledged full restitution.

**Fund Flow**  
BitBank → Iran Revolutionary Guard (BTC transfer); internal custodial account transfers within Blink Wallet.
