### 1. Startale — Hack / Exploit

- **Source**: [Startale — Smart Contract Vulnerability](https://x.com/exvulsec/status/2100111910524072258) (SlowMist Hacked, 2026-09-16)
- **Incident date**: 2026-09-16  |  **Chains**: Ethereum  |  **Amount**: $2.9K
- **Tags**: Access Control, Improper Access Control, Smart Contract Vulnerability
- **Other sources**: [DeFiLlama Hacks](https://defillama.com/hacks)

**Background**  
Startale is a project that offers smart accounts built on the ERC-7579 standard. These accounts use an initialization flag during factory deployment. The vulnerability allowed the flag to persist for the whole transaction, enabling re-initialization. An attacker leveraged this to drain about 330 pre-funded counterfactual accounts.

**Attack / Modus Operandi**  
The attacker abused the transient-storage initialization flag in initializeAccount to re-initialize the contract with a malicious bootstrap within the same transaction.

**Summary**  
On Sep 16, 2026, Startale’s ERC-7579 smart accounts were exploited, resulting in a loss of roughly $2,876. The attacker reused the initialization flag to empty 330 pre-funded counterfactual accounts. The loss stemmed solely from the pre-funded accounts, and the Soneium network remained unaffected. The project is working on remediation.

### 2. Flamincome — Hack / Exploit

- **Source**: [Flamincome — Smart Contract Vulnerability](https://x.com/SlowMist_Team/status/2100256590716907706) (SlowMist Hacked, 2026-09-16)
- **Incident date**: 2026-09-16  |  **Chains**: Ethereum  |  **Amount**: $345.9K
- **Tags**: Smart Contract Vulnerability

**Background**  
Flamincome is the legacy smart-contract suite of Flamingo Finance that runs a vault for YUSDT and a strategy module. The contracts treat the balance of a Convex BaseRewardPool as its own assets and value it using Curve’s get_virtual_price(). Because asset accounting and valuation are unsafe, external LP tokens can be injected. This design flaw enabled the exploit.

**Attack / Modus Operandi**  
The attacker flash-loaned roughly $18M of USDT, injected a USDP/3CRV LP into the strategy, and over-valued the Convex BaseRewardPool balance as assets.

**Summary**  
On Sep 16 2026 the attacker used a flash loan to inject LP, inflating the VaultYUSDT share price. They then redeemed real Aave aUSDT liquidity, profiting about $345.9K. The reported loss to Flamincome was $345,900. SlowMist disclosed the incident and no further losses have been reported.

**Fund Flow**  
Flash-loaned USDT → LP injected into strategy → VaultYUSDT price inflated → Aave aUSDT liquidity withdrawn → $345.9K profit.
