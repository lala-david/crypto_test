### 1. Coinbase — Law Enforcement

- **Source**: [United States Attorney's Office Files Civil Forfeiture Action to Recover Cryptocurrency Involved in an Account Takeover Fraud Scheme](https://www.justice.gov/usao-ma/pr/united-states-attorneys-office-files-civil-forfeiture-action-recover-cryptocurrency-0) (US DOJ, 2026-09-28)
- **Incident date**: 2026-09-28  |  **Chains**: unknown  |  **Amount**: $47.0K
- **Tags**: USAO - Massachusetts, 정책
- **Other sources**: [rss:tokenpost](https://www.tokenpost.kr/news/policy/415414)

**Background**  
The victim was an individual residing in Massachusetts who used a Coinbase account. Scammers sent text messages pretending to be “Coinbase” and obtained the victim’s login credentials. The stolen funds were transferred from the Coinbase account to an account at the Biance exchange.

**Attack / Modus Operandi**  
Phishing via fake Coinbase texts to steal login credentials, followed by account takeover and fund transfer.

**Summary**  
On Sep 28, 2026 the U.S. Attorney’s Office filed a civil forfeiture action to recover 110,270 USDT (≈$47K). The USDT is alleged to be proceeds of the account-takeover fraud. Funds moved from the victim’s Coinbase account to a Biance exchange account and were seized in August 2026. The forfeiture will allow the government to recover the assets and return them to the victim.

**Fund Flow**  
Coinbase account → Biance exchange account (transfer) → seized in Aug 2026.

- **Other sources**: [rss:blockmedia](https://www.blockmedia.co.kr/archives/1145367), [DeFiLlama Hacks](https://defillama.com/hacks)

**Background**  
DYORSWAP is a multi-chain DEX that added a network labeled GIWA Mainnet on Sep 27 2026. The network was a counterfeit chain built by scammers with a bogus bridge. Users deposited Ethereum-mainnet ETH into this bridge. The operators later drained about 766.25 ETH.

**Attack / Modus Operandi**  
The scam used a fake chain and a fraudulent bridge to lure users into depositing real ETH, after which the operators siphoned the bridge funds.

**Summary**  
On Sep 27 2026 DYORSWAP listed a network presented as GIWA Mainnet. Users deposited ETH into its fake bridge, and the operators drained about 766.25 ETH (≈$2M). DYORSWAP stated its contracts were not exploited and has begun compensating victims, already paying over 200 ETH. Tracing of the stolen funds is ongoing.

**Fund Flow**  
User → fake bridge (operator) → 766.25 ETH stolen; DYORSWAP → victims (200+ ETH compensation).

**Attack / Modus Operandi**
