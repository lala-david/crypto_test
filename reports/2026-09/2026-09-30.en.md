**Background**  
Bitget is a global cryptocurrency exchange. In early September 2026 about $387.5 million of assets were stolen in a hack. After the breach, fraudsters pretended to be investment-firm contacts and approached the CEO. The scheme leveraged the recent hack to lure the executive.

**Attack / Modus Operandi**  
Scammers impersonated investment-firm representatives and sent a cooperation proposal to the CEO, a classic social-engineering fraud.

**Summary**  
Four days after the hack, scammers posing as investment-firm staff offered cooperation to Bitget’s CEO. The CEO disclosed the attempt in an interview. The perpetrators remain unidentified and no further loss has been reported.

### 2. Cryptoaiml Ltd., Cryptoaiml Capital Foundation, TSAI Pro Ltd., TSAI Capital Foundation — Scam / Fraud

- **Source**: [1500만달러 사기 혐의…SEC, 왓츠앱 이용한 법인 4곳 제소](https://www.tokenpost.kr/news/policy/415970) (rss:tokenpost, 2026-09-29)
- **Incident date**: 2026-09-29  |  **Chains**: unknown  |  **Amount**: $15.00M ($15 million)
- **Actors**: Cryptoaiml Ltd., Cryptoaiml Capital Foundation, TSAI Pro Ltd., TSAI Capital Foundation
- **Tags**: 정책

**Background**  
The U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) is cracking down on crypto investment scams. Cryptoaiml Ltd. and its foundation, as well as TSAI Pro Ltd. and its foundation, impersonated investment experts in WhatsApp group chats from Aug 2024 to Mar 2025. They provided AI-generated false investment signals and promised high returns. They lured investors to a fake cryptocurrency trading platform and stole funds.

**Attack / Modus Operandi**  
They pretended to be investment experts and used AI-generated signals to promise high returns, deceiving investors. Victims transferred money to a bogus trading platform.

**Summary**  
On Sep 29 2026 the SEC filed civil lawsuits in the Southern District of New York against four entities. The companies are accused of defrauding investors of over $15 million. The scheme used WhatsApp and Facebook to lure victims and a fake crypto platform to collect funds. The cases are ongoing, seeking restitution and damages.
