Former Los Angeles County Sheriff’s Department deputy Christopher Michael Cadman used his position to threaten and intimidate opponents of cryptocurrency businessman Adam Iza. In August 2021 Cadman and another deputy held a victim at gunpoint in Iza’s Bel Air mansion and later arranged a traffic stop to further harass the same individual. **Summary**

The court ordered him to pay $25,000 in restitution. Cadman had threatened Iza’s adversary, forcing the victim to send $25,000 to Iza’s account, and he omitted $40,500 of income on his 2021 tax filing. Prosecutors highlighted the abuse of police authority and fraudulent gain. Cadman is now incarcerated.

### 2. SBA Paycheck Protection Program — Law Enforcement

- **Source**: [Eastern District of Virginia joins DOJ Fraud Division, SBA, and SBA OIG in surge takedown exceeding $245M in COVID-era loan fraud](https://www.justice.gov/usao-edva/pr/eastern-district-virginia-joins-doj-fraud-division-sba-and-sba-oig-surge-takedown) (US DOJ, 2026-09-14)
- **Incident date**: unknown  |  **Chains**: unknown  |  **Amount**: $245.00M ($245 million)
- **Actors**: Karen J. DeMatteo, Tae Young Q. Jang, Sheila Bynum-Coleman
- **Tags**: National Fraud Enforcement Division, USAO - Virginia, Eastern

**Background**  
The Paycheck Protection Program (PPP) provided payroll assistance to small businesses during the COVID-19 pandemic. Federal authorities issued loans through the program, and fraudsters submitted false business and payroll information. This enforcement action was a joint effort by the DOJ and SBA to combat large-scale fraud.

**Attack / Modus Operandi**  
Defendants created fictitious businesses and inflated payroll and revenue figures to obtain loans. They also used identity theft and concealed foreign ties to misappropriate funds.

**Summary**  
On Sep 14, 2026, the Eastern District of Virginia announced a $245M PPP fraud takedown involving over 160 defendants. Key defendants caused losses of $2.125M, $225K and $17K respectively. Misappropriated funds were used to buy cryptocurrency, real estate and a car. Courts sentenced the defendants to prison, and the government is pursuing fund recovery and prevention measures.

**Fund Flow**  
PPP/EIDL loan proceeds were spent on cryptocurrency purchases, housing and vehicle acquisitions.
