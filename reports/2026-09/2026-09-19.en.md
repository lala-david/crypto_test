### 1. RISEx — Hack / Exploit

- **Source**: [RISEx, 탈취금 20% 남기고 80% 반환 요구…23일 시한](https://www.tokenpost.kr/news/blockchain/410745) (rss:tokenpost, 2026-09-19)
- **Incident date**: unknown  |  **Chains**: Ethereum, Base  |  **Amount**: $673.0K
- **Tags**: 블록체인

**Background**  
RISEx is a decentralized exchange operating on Ethereum and Base networks. The XLP vault is a smart contract that offers real-world asset (RWA) strategies. In August 2026 a configuration error made the vault vulnerable to unauthorized withdrawals. An attacker stole 673,011.56 USDC from the vault.

**Attack / Modus Operandi**  
The attacker exploited the vault’s misconfiguration to execute an unapproved withdrawal. The contract’s permission checks were bypassed.

**Summary**  
In August 2026, RISEx’s XLP vault suffered an unauthorized withdrawal of 673,011.56 USDC. RISEx told the attacker to keep about 13,456.6 USDC and return the remaining 80% by Sep 23. The return must be sent to a designated address on Ethereum or Base. If the attacker complies, RISEx will treat the case as a white-hat resolution. The status of the repayment is currently unknown.

**Fund Flow**  
The attacker currently holds roughly 13,456.6 USDC. They must send the remaining 538,265 USDC or equivalent ETH to the specified address.

### 2. Radix Engine — Hack / Exploit

- **Source**: [[오후 뉴스브리핑] 래딕스 취약점으로 126만달러 탈취 外](https://www.tokenpost.kr/news/briefing/410679) (rss:tokenpost, 2026-09-19)
- **Incident date**: 2026-09-17  |  **Chains**: Ethereum, BSC, Solana, Radix  |  **Amount**: $1.26M ($1.26 million)
- **Tags**: 브리핑

**Background**  
The Radix Engine is the core execution layer of the Radix network. It manages vault contracts that hold and release tokens after ownership verification. A flaw in the vault ownership check allowed an attacker to withdraw assets.

**Attack / Modus Operandi**  
The attacker exploited the vault ownership verification flaw to withdraw ETH, WBTC, USDT, USDC, BNB, SOL and XRD.

**Summary**  
On Sep 17, 2026, a vulnerability in the Radix Engine was exploited, resulting in the theft of about $1.26M worth of assets. The stolen tokens spanned Ethereum, BNB Chain, Solana and Radix networks. The attack halted network transaction processing. Radix Foundation responded by blocking the hyperlane path and moving some validator stakes offline to prevent further abuse. Investigation and recovery efforts are ongoing.

**Fund Flow**  
Assets withdrawn from third-party vaults were sent to attacker addresses and may have been moved across chains via bridges.

### 3. Osmosis — Hack / Exploit

- **Source**: [Nomic - Rekt](https://rekt.news/nomic-rekt) (rekt.news, 2026-09-18)
- **Incident date**: 2026-06-25  |  **Chains**: Cosmos, Ethereum, Noble, Axelar  |  **Amount**: $3.15M ($3.15 million)
- **Tags**: Nomic, Osmosis, Rekt

**Background**  
Nomic is a Cosmos-SDK chain that mints nBTC tokens against Bitcoin deposits via IBC. Its ibc_deliver function contained a flaw that allowed a double-mint for a single deposit. An attacker exploited this to create 40.65 nBTC (≈40 BTC) without any Bitcoin backing. The counterfeit nBTC was transferred to Osmosis’s allBTC transmuter and caused a $3.15M shortfall.

**Attack / Modus Operandi**  
The attacker triggered the vulnerable ibc_deliver function to mint nBTC twice for one Bitcoin deposit, sending the duplicate tokens via IBC to Osmosis as false vouchers. The counterfeit nBTC was swapped 1:1 for allBTC, bridged through Axelar and SquidRouter to Ethereum, and then laundered through Tornado Cash.

**Summary**  
On June 25 2026 the ibc_deliver bug in Nomic minted 40.65 nBTC without Bitcoin backing. The counterfeit tokens were swapped 1:1 for allBTC on Osmosis, creating a $3.15M shortfall. The attacker moved roughly 18 BTC worth of value across chains and into Tornado Cash. The remaining 22.65 BTC-equivalent was frozen by Osmosis on Sep 7 via an emergency upgrade. The unfrozen funds remain unrecovered pending governance action.

**Fund Flow**  
Unbacked nBTC was swapped to allBTC on Osmosis, then routed through Axelar to WBTC/ETH on Ethereum via the SquidRouter, and finally sent to Tornado Cash. About 18 BTC worth was laundered through Tornado Cash, while the remaining amount was frozen in the Osmosis address.

### 4. AI Bot Lecture Scam — Scam / Fraud

- **Source**: [274.60 ETH 탈취한 AI 봇 강의…224명 직접 배포](https://www.tokenpost.kr/news/blockchain/410502) (rss:tokenpost, 2026-09-18)

- **Tags**: 블록체인
- **Follow-up — first reported**: [2026-09-16 · Fake AI Trading Bot Drainer](https://www.trmlabs.com/resources/blog/fake-ai-trading-bots-are-getting-victims-to-build-their-own-drainers)

**Background**  
A fraud group posed as an AI trading-bot lecture and urged victims to deploy their own smart contracts. 224 victims deposited ETH into those contracts, a method different from typical phishing.

**Attack / Modus Operandi**  
Malicious code in the 234 victim-deployed contracts siphoned ETH, stealing a total of 274.60 ETH.

**Summary**  
From Feb 12 to Aug 11 2026 a total of 274.60 ETH (≈$517,205) was stolen. The perpetrators funneled the funds into six controlled addresses. TRM Labs reported the case on Sep 14. The addresses are under investigation and further action is required.

**Fund Flow**  
Victim contracts → six attacker-controlled addresses.
